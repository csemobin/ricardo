## 7. Booking and Ride Services

### 7.1 Requesting a Ride

When a Rider submits a ride request, the Platform identifies nearby available Drivers using real-time GPS and sends them the request via push notification. Lyfuber does not guarantee Driver availability in every area at all times.

### 7.2 Driver Matching

Riders and Drivers are matched algorithmically based on GPS proximity, Driver availability, and estimated arrival time. Lyfuber makes no guarantee regarding the speed or outcome of any match.

### 7.3 Ride Confirmation

When a Driver accepts a request, both the Rider and the Driver receive confirmation. The Rider can view the Driver's name, profile photo, vehicle details, and live GPS position on the map. The ride is considered confirmed from this point.

### 7.4 During the Ride

GPS location for both the Rider's trip and the Driver's vehicle is tracked throughout the active ride session for navigation, safety, and fare verification purposes. All location data usage is described in full in our Privacy Policy.

### 7.5 Ride Completion

A ride is marked complete when the Driver confirms arrival at the destination. The applicable fare is immediately charged to the Rider's wallet or payment card. The Rider is then prompted to rate the Driver and may optionally leave a tip.

### 7.6 Safety

Safety is our top priority. Either party may report safety concerns, unsafe behavior, or incidents through the App at any time. In any emergency, **always contact local emergency services (911 or your local equivalent) first**. Lyfuber's support team reviews all submitted reports and takes appropriate action.

## 8. Pricing, Payments, and Wallet

### 8.1 Fare Calculation

Fares are calculated on a per-mile basis based on the Driver's rate, subject to any Platform-level minimums, adjustments, or applicable taxes. A fare estimate is shown to the Rider before booking. Final fares may differ from estimates due to traffic, route changes, detours, or wait time.

### 8.2 In-App Wallet

Lyfuber provides an in-app wallet for both Riders and Drivers:

- **Riders** use the wallet to pay for rides
- **Drivers** receive their earnings into the wallet and may request bank withdrawals

Wallet balances are maintained server-side. Balances have no monetary value outside the Platform (except Driver withdrawal requests).

### 8.3 Adding Funds

Riders top up their wallet through the in-app payment gateway — a secure page processed by our third-party payment processor displayed within the App's WebView. Lyfuber does not store full card numbers, CVV codes, or raw card data on its servers.

### 8.4 Tips

Riders may voluntarily send tips to Drivers after a completed ride. Tips are deducted from the Rider's wallet and credited in full to the Driver.

### 8.5 Driver Earnings and Withdrawals

Drivers earn a per-mile rate for each completed ride. Earnings are credited to the in-app wallet. Drivers may request a withdrawal to their registered bank account. Minimum withdrawal amounts, processing timelines, and any applicable fees are defined in the withdrawal settings within the App and may be updated periodically.

### 8.6 Taxes

You are solely responsible for all taxes applicable to your transactions on the Platform. Lyfuber does not withhold, collect, or remit taxes on your behalf unless required to do so by law.

### 8.7 Payment Disputes

## 12. Location Services

### 12.1 Why Location Access Is Required

Lyfuber's core functionality depends on device GPS. Without location access the App cannot: match Riders with nearby Drivers, display the map, calculate ride fares, provide navigation, or verify ride completion.

### 12.2 Foreground Location Service (Android)

On Android, the App runs a **foreground location service** during active ride sessions. A **persistent, visible notification** is shown on your device for the entire duration this service is active, informing you that location is being collected — as required by Google Play's location data policy and Android platform rules.

This foreground service:
- Is active **only during an active ride** — started when a ride begins, stopped when it ends
- Requires the permissions: `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION`, `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`, `FOREGROUND_SERVICE`, `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION`
- Is declared in the Android Manifest with `foregroundServiceType="location"`

### 12.3 No Background Location Outside Rides

We do not track your location when no ride is in progress. There is no silent or passive background location collection at any other time.

### 12.4 iOS Location Permission

On iOS, the App requests **"When In Use"** location access for Riders during active sessions. Drivers may be asked for **"Always"** location access so the App can broadcast their availability while backgrounded.

### 12.5 Driver Location Sharing

When a Driver goes online, their GPS position is periodically shared with nearby Riders browsing the map. During a matched ride, the Driver's real-time location is visible exclusively to that matched Rider.

### 12.6 Revoking Location Permission

You can revoke location permission at any time through device Settings. Core features will not work without active location access.
