3. LOCATION DATA COLLECTION AND USE

Location data is essential to the Lyfuber service. We are committed to 
collecting only what is necessary and being fully transparent about our 
practices.

3.1 WHY WE NEED YOUR LOCATION

Precise GPS location enables us to:
  • Show your current position on the map
  • Match you with nearby Drivers
  • Display real-time Driver location during rides
  • Calculate accurate trip distances and fares
  • Provide turn-by-turn navigation
  • Verify ride completion at the correct destination

3.2 WHEN WE COLLECT LOCATION DATA

For Riders:
  • During active ride sessions only
  • We do not track your location when you are not using the app for a ride

For Drivers:
  • When you are online and available to accept rides
  • During active ride sessions
  • We do not track your location when you are offline

3.3 HOW WE COLLECT LOCATION

On Android:
  • We use a foreground location service during active rides
  • A persistent notification is displayed whenever location tracking is active
  • This notification clearly states that your location is being collected
  • The service stops automatically when your ride ends

On iOS:
  • Riders: We request "When In Use" location permission
  • Drivers: We may request "Always Allow" to receive ride requests while the 
    app is in the background

3.4 LOCATION DATA SECURITY

All location data is transmitted using industry-standard encryption. Location 
information is shared only between matched Riders and Drivers during active 
rides, and is not retained beyond the ride session except as necessary for 
fare calculation and safety purposes.

You may revoke location permissions at any time through your device settings, 
though this will prevent the app from functioning properly.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We share information

These providers are contractually obligated to protect your information and 
use it only for the specific services they provide to us.

5. YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS AND CHOICES

You have rights regarding your personal information. The specific rights 
available to you may depend on where you live.

5.1 ACCESS AND CORRECTION
You can view and update most of your information directly in the app. For a 
complete copy of your data, contact us at support@gogodriver.us.

5.2 ACCOUNT DELETION
You may permanently delete your account at any time:
  • In the app: Settings → Delete Account
  • By email: Send a request to support@gogodriver.us with subject "Account 
    Deletion Request"

When you delete your account:
  • Your profile and personal information will be removed within 90 days
  • Certain data may be retained as required by law (see Section 7)
  • Your authentication sessions are terminated immediately
  • Deleted accounts cannot be recovered

Before deleting, please withdraw any remaining wallet balance, as it may be 
forfeited upon deletion.

5.3 DATA PORTABILITY
You may request a copy of your personal data in a machine-readable format by 
contacting us at support@gogodriver.us.

5.4 RESTRICT PROCESSING
You may request that we limit how we process your data in certain circumstances, 
such as while investigating a complaint.

5.5 WITHDRAW CONSENT
You may withdraw consent for location tracking or push notifications at any 
time through your device settings. Note that this may limit app functionality.

5.6 OPT-OUT OF MARKETING
We do not use your information for third-party marketing. You may opt out of 
our own promotional communications by adjusting your notification preferences.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@gogodriver.us. We will 
respond within 30 days of receiving a verified request.

7. DATA RETENTION

We retain your information only as long as necessary to provide our services 
and comply with legal obligations:

  • Account data: Duration of active account plus 90 days after deletion
  • Ride history: Up to 3 years
  • Payment records: Up to 7 years (required by financial regulations)
  • Driver verification documents: Active period plus 1 year
  • Location data: Not retained beyond the associated ride session
  • Safety reports: Up to 5 years
  • Support communications: Up to 3 years
  • Diagnostic data: Up to 12 months

After these periods, data is securely deleted or anonymized. Retained data is 
never used for marketing or profiling.

8. DATA SECURITY

We take data security seriously and implement multiple layers of protection:

  • Encryption: All data transmitted between the app and our servers is 
    encrypted using industry-standard protocols
  • Password Security: Passwords are hashed using strong one-way algorithms and 
    never stored in plain text
  • Secure Authentication: Account access requires verification through secure 
    tokens and one-time password codes
  • Payment Security: Card information is processed by PCI-DSS compliant 
    payment processors. We never store complete card numbers or security codes
  • Access Controls: Access to user data is restricted to authorized personnel 
    with legitimate business needs
  • Regular Security Audits: We continuously monitor and improve our security 
    measures

While we implement strong safeguards, no system is completely immune to 
security risks. If we become aware of a data breach, we will notify affected 
users and relevant authorities as required by law.

12. STATE-SPECIFIC PRIVACY RIGHTS

12.1 CALIFORNIA RESIDENTS (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the 
California Consumer Privacy Act:

Right to Know: You may request information about the personal data we collect, 
use, disclose, and retain.

Right to Delete: You may request deletion of your personal information (see 
Section 5.2).

Right to Correct: You may request correction of inaccurate information.

Right to Opt-Out: We do not sell your personal information. We do not share 
it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Right to Limit Use of Sensitive Data: We use precise location data only to 
provide transportation services, not for advertising or profiling.

We recognize and honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals as required by 
California law.

Categories of Personal Information Collected (Last 12 Months):
  • Identifiers (name, email, phone, device ID)
  • Precise geolocation (GPS coordinates during rides)
  • Financial information (wallet balance, transaction history)
  • Visual information (profile photos, Driver documents)
  • Inferences (ratings, completion rates)

To exercise your California privacy rights, contact support@gogodriver.us.

12.2 EUROPEAN RESIDENTS (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or 
Switzerland, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) grants you 
specific rights:

Lyfuber acts as the Data Controller for your personal data.

Legal Bases for Processing:
  • Contract Performance: Account creation, ride matching, payments
  • Legitimate Interests: Safety, fraud prevention, platform improvement
  • Legal Obligation: Financial record-keeping, regulatory compliance
  • Consent: Push notifications, certain location tracking

Your GDPR Rights:
  • Right to access your personal data
  • Right to rectification of inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to restrict processing
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to object to processing
  • Right to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority

To exercise your GDPR rights, contact support@gogodriver.us.
