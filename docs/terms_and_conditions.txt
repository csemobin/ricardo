6. RIDE SERVICES

6.1 REQUESTING RIDES

Riders request rides through the app. The Platform uses GPS to identify nearby 
available Drivers and sends them ride requests via push notification. Lyfuber 
does not guarantee Driver availability at all times or in all locations.

6.2 MATCHING AND CONFIRMATION

Riders and Drivers are matched algorithmically based on proximity, 
availability, and other factors. Once a Driver accepts, both parties receive 
confirmation and can view relevant information about each other.

6.3 DURING THE RIDE

GPS location is tracked during active rides for navigation, safety, and fare 
calculation. All location data usage is described in our Privacy Policy.

6.4 RIDE COMPLETION

Rides are marked complete when the Driver confirms arrival at the destination. 
Fares are immediately charged to the Rider's selected payment method. Riders 
are prompted to rate the Driver and may optionally leave a tip.

6.5 SAFETY

Either party may report safety concerns through the app. All reports are 
reviewed by our support team.

EMERGENCY: In any safety emergency, always contact local emergency services 
(911 or your local equivalent) BEFORE contacting Lyfuber support.

7. PRICING AND PAYMENTS

7.1 FARES

Fares are calculated per mile based on Driver rates, subject to Platform 
minimums and applicable taxes. Fare estimates are shown before booking but 
may differ from final charges due to traffic, route changes, or wait time.

7.2 IN-APP WALLET

Lyfuber provides an in-app wallet for payments:
  • Riders add funds using accepted payment methods through our secure payment 
    gateway
  • Drivers receive earnings into their wallet and may request withdrawals
  • Wallet balances are maintained server-side and associated with your account

7.3 PAYMENT CARDS

Riders may save payment cards for convenience. Card data is processed by our 
PCI-DSS compliant payment processor. We do not store complete card numbers 
or security codes on our servers.

7.4 TIPS

Riders may voluntarily send tips to Drivers after rides. Tips are credited 
in full to the Driver.

7.5 DRIVER EARNINGS AND WITHDRAWALS

Drivers earn per-mile rates for completed rides. Earnings may be withdrawn 
to registered bank accounts subject to minimum amounts and processing times 
defined in the app.

7.6 TAXES

You are solely responsible for determining and paying all taxes applicable to 
your Platform transactions. Lyfuber does not withhold or remit taxes unless 
required by law.

7.7 PAYMENT DISPUTES

If you believe a charge is incorrect, contact us at support@gogodriver.us 
within 30 days. Initiating a chargeback with your bank before contacting us
